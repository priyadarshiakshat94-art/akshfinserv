Plabita started Aksh Finserv in 1998 from a small office in Powai. The first clients were neighbours and colleagues who wanted someone to explain their insurance papers in plain language. Twenty-eight years later, more than 1,500 families work with us, and she still sits in on every first review.

Her rule is simple: no product gets recommended until we know what the money is for. She has been named to MDRT Top of the Table, the AWMIE Top 100 Women in Finance list in 2019, and Kotak Life's Living Legend recognition. She will tell you the award she values most is the number of clients whose children now have their own folders with us.
