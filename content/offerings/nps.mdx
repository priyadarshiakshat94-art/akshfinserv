## How we do it

First, we read your salary structure. If your employer offers NPS and you are not using it, that is usually the fastest tax saving available to you.

Next, we set the equity share by age and revisit it every year. NPS sits inside your retirement plan, next to EPF, PPF and mutual funds, never on its own.

Ten years before 60, we start the annuity conversation. The insurer, the annuity type and the lump sum split are decided with numbers on paper, not on the day the account matures.
