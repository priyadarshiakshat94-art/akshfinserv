## How we do it

We run two numbers before anything else: what redeeming would cost you in tax and lost growth, and what borrowing would cost in interest. If redemption is cheaper, we tell you.

If a loan makes sense, we choose which funds to pledge, keep the amount well under the lender's limit, and set a repayment date on day one.

A loan against funds is a bridge. We make sure it has an end.
