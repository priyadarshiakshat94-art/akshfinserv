## How we do it

We start with dates, not funds. Retirement in 2041. College in 2032. A flat down payment in 2029. Each date gets its own bucket.

Short dates get debt and hybrid funds. Long dates get equity. As a date gets closer, we move money down the risk ladder every year, so a market fall in the final stretch cannot derail the goal.

Then we pick funds. We look for consistent process, reasonable cost, and managers who do what they said they would do. We avoid owning the same 30 stocks through five different schemes.

Every six months, we send you one page: each goal, the money set aside for it, and whether it is on track.
