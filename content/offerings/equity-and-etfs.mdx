## How we do it

We cap direct equity at a share of your portfolio that you could watch fall 40% without changing your life. For most clients that is 10% to 20%.

The core is built with low-cost index ETFs. The satellite, if you want one, is yours to run, and we track it inside the same goal review so you see the whole picture.

For concentrated holdings, we set a sell-down schedule in writing. Selling on a schedule beats selling on a feeling.
