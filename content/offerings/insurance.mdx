## How we do it

We keep insurance and investment apart. Term cover protects income. Health cover protects savings. Investments go in investment products.

For existing policies, we read every document you hold and list what each one covers, what it costs and when it ends. Gaps and overlaps become obvious on one page.

When a claim comes, you call us, not a helpline. We file it, chase it and stay on it until it is paid.
